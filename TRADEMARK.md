## Forks and derivative projects

You are welcome to fork, modify, self-host, and redistribute the source code under the terms of the [AGPL-3.0](./LICENSE). However, if you publish or distribute a fork, custom build, hosted or self-hosted service, website, package, app store listing, or other derivative project:

- **Use your own name and branding.** Remove and replace the Capgo name, wordmark, logos, application icons, and other Capgo branding with your own.
- **Make it clear that your project is not Capgo.** Do not use the Capgo name, wordmark, logo, or other branding in a way that suggests your project is Capgo, is an official Capgo product or service, or is affiliated with, endorsed by, or maintained by Digital Shift OU. State clearly that your project is unofficial and not endorsed by Capgo.
- **Keep references factual.** You may include a truthful, plain-text attribution noting that your project is derived from this repository (for example, "An unofficial fork based on the Capgo source code"), provided you do not use Capgo logos or wordmarks, do not adopt "Capgo" as your project's name or domain, and clearly state that your project is unofficial.

Running your own private, internal self-hosted instance for your team is fine. These rules apply when you present a fork or service to others.
